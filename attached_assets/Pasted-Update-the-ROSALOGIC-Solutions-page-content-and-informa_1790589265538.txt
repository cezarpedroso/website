## ROSALOGIC POSITIONING

ROSALOGIC is a software engineering company that designs and builds custom business systems for operational businesses.

The primary offering is CUSTOM BUSINESS SOFTWARE.

Integration, data, infrastructure, security, deployment, and support are supporting capabilities that exist around the software systems we build.

The company should NOT appear to be:
- a general IT provider
- a cybersecurity consultancy
- an industrial automation company
- a generic digital agency
- a data engineering consultancy
- a giant enterprise consulting firm

The positioning should communicate:

"Software built around the way your business works."

A useful description of the company:

"ROSALOGIC designs and builds custom business software for organizations whose operations have outgrown spreadsheets, disconnected tools, or off-the-shelf software that doesn't quite fit."

The target customer is primarily small and mid-sized operational businesses that are too complex for generic software but do not have a large internal engineering team.

Examples include:
- manufacturing
- agriculture
- logistics and transportation
- professional services
- other operational SMBs

Do not imply that ROSALOGIC only serves these industries.
