---
name: Home and Solutions copy separation
description: Why the homepage teaser and detailed Solutions hero must remain distinct
---

Keep the homepage's Solutions teaser distinct from the detailed Solutions page. If a brief mandates exact Solutions hero wording, preserve that wording on the Solutions page and use different introductory language on the homepage.

**Why:** The user explicitly separated the homepage's brand-introduction role from the Solutions page's commercial explanation. Repeating the same headline and introductory copy on both pages undermined that distinction.

**How to apply:** When editing either page's opening, compare it with the other page and preserve each page's separate job without weakening required Solutions copy.
