---
name: Contact-channel integrity
description: How to describe contact actions while a verified destination is unavailable
---

Treat the Contact page as informational until a verified inbox or other delivery channel is supplied. Navigation can lead to the page, but calls to action should not imply that a visitor can send a message when they cannot.

**Why:** Contact delivery is awaiting confirmed recipient details. An outreach promise without a working destination misleads visitors.

**How to apply:** When editing contact buttons, page copy, metadata, or forms, avoid invented addresses and send promises. Once a verified channel is connected, update the action and its surrounding language together.
